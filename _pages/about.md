I'm a Senior Software Engineer and software testing specialist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Computational Mathematics from the University of São Paulo (USP). My work sits at the intersection of software engineering and software quality: building reliable backend systems, hardening cloud infrastructure, and automating testing activities.

## Today

- **Senior Software Engineer at Elation** (remote, since 2026). I'm the technical lead on the SOC 2 compliance effort: hardening AWS infrastructure, setting up security monitoring (GuardDuty, Security Hub, CloudTrail, AWS Config), managing vulnerabilities, and writing security policies. I also do backend development with TypeScript/NestJS.
- **Tech Lead at NEES/UFAL** (remote, since 2022). I lead a development team building educational platforms for the Brazilian Ministry of Education (MEC/FNDE) and the Ministry of Culture. The work covers AWS-based microservices, large-scale data processing, and quality engineering. I joined NEES as a Senior Test Engineer before moving into the tech lead role.

## Background

I was born in Brazil in 1987 and grew up in Teresina, the capital of Piauí. I earned my bachelor's degree in Information Systems at Faculdade Integral Diferencial (FACID) in 2012 and worked on Java backend systems during and after my studies.

In 2013 I moved to São Carlos to start a master's at USP. There I researched parallel execution of programs to reduce the cost of **mutation testing**. My Ph.D. (2017–2022, funded by FAPESP) proposed an approach for testing **virtual reality applications** with **metamorphic testing**, using deep reinforcement learning to generate test data automatically.

## Research

I'm still active in academia as a member of the Software Engineering Laboratory (LabES/ICMC-USP) and a reviewer for venues such as *STVR*, *IJSEKE*, *ICST*, the *International Workshop on Metamorphic Testing*, and *SAST*. My interests include:

- automated software testing and test oracles;
- mutation testing and metamorphic testing;
- machine learning and AI agents applied to testing;
- software testing education and empirical software engineering.
